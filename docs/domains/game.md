# RPG and game systems

## Boundary

Virtual progression never modifies real money. Character experience, levels, equipment and virtual rewards are game state, never financial assets or balance adjustments. Game has no Finance write capability.

Financial facts may qualify a mission or progression rule only through authorized server contracts. Mission completion does not prove that a real payment occurred. A user acknowledgement is not settlement evidence. Game consumes committed facts, never tentative client displays or model assertions.

The server owns persisted missions, completion and progression. Rule versions and triggering evidence identify why rewards were granted. Replayed evidence/retried requests must not grant duplicate rewards. Financial corrections require an explicit progression reconciliation policy before implementing rewards tied to that fact; never rewrite financial history to preserve a reward.

## Design baseline

The avatar represents the user. Character presentation may personalize appearance without inferring financial facts, gender or career from appearance. Offer male and female character directions under the [art baseline](../art-direction.md).

Mission mechanics translate financial-health goals into understandable actions. Present financial amounts and estimates using their financial labels; fantasy names cannot obscure obligations, debt, reserve targets or forecast status. Advice from Estrategista may propose a mission, but deterministic validated game commands establish its rules, evidence and reward.

No real-money reward, purchase, trading or monetization mechanic is approved by this foundation. Progression formulas, mission taxonomy, eligibility, pacing, failure/expiry and correction policy remain product decisions for the Game PR. Do not introduce engagement mechanics that punish financial hardship without explicit product review.
