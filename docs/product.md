# Product constitution

Forja is a mobile financial-health RPG. The real user is represented by an anime-style character; real financial life supplies evidence for progression. The experience feels like an RPG while keeping financial information highly readable.

Product scope: balances; income and expenses; future debits; receivables; debts; emergency reserve; financial goals; required daily/hourly income; work/career profile; missions; progression; daily, weekly, monthly and custom reports. Scope does not establish formulas, integration providers or permission to implement features.

The three advisory character roles and their operational contracts are defined in the [AI domain](domains/ai.md); visual treatment belongs to [art direction](art-direction.md).

Financial truth is governed by the [financial domain](domains/financial.md), game consequences by the [game domain](domains/game.md). Progression supports financial health without implying that a game reward is money or that advice is an executed action.

Product decisions still needed: target audience and initial jurisdiction, supported currencies, account sources and reconciliation, launch features, revenue model, report interpretation and notification policy. Decide only the subset needed by the current PR. LGPD requirements are owned by [security](security.md).
